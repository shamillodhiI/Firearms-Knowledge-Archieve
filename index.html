      About this project

      About & Disclaimer

      Purpose

      The Firearms Knowledge Archive is a historical and educational reference covering categories, manufacturers, notable models and terminology. It is designed to grow: every entry is plain data that can be edited without changing the design.

      Content policy

      • No construction, manufacturing or modification guidance
      • No guidance on defeating safety features or increasing lethality
      • No acquisition, evasion or tactical-use content
      • Descriptive terminology only

      Accuracy & classification

      • Specifications vary by production variant
      • Uncertain details are labelled “Information requires verification”
      • Russian makers are grouped under Europe (Izhevsk and Tula lie in European Russia)
      • Israel and Türkiye are grouped as “Middle East & Türkiye”
      • Browning is listed under the United States (Utah origin) with Belgian/FN production noted

      Disclaimer. This website is intended for historical, educational and informational purposes. Information is presented as a reference and should not be interpreted as instructions for constructing, modifying, acquiring, or using firearms.
